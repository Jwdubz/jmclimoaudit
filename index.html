What I found

01

Your homepage downloads 119 images and loads slowly on a phone-size test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, your homepage came to 9.8 MB with 119 image files. The first content appeared at 2.9 seconds, but the largest part, the logo image at the top, didn't finish until 9.7 and 10.1 seconds on two runs (speed scores 47 and 46). The test estimates that sizing the photos to the space they fill would save about 7.9 MB, and the largest single files are the fleet photos (274, 231 and 220 KB). Your Fleet page scored 51 (largest part at 7.8 seconds) and your Video Gallery 68 (5.0 seconds). One test setup; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your pages arrive empty, and the words appear only after a script runs.

The page the server sends for your homepage is 1,772 bytes with no words in it: a title and a script, but no heading, description, phone number or address. Everything a visitor reads is drawn afterwards by that script, and the title then changes from “JMC Limousine - Luxury Transportation in Las Vegas” to “Luxury Limo Service Las Vegas | JMC Limousine”. A browser and the search tool I used both read it, and the Cookies, Privacy and Terms pages never get the second title. A tool that reads only the page the server sends gets nothing from any page. I didn't test individual AI assistants.

03

Nineteen of your 21 pages share one description, and eight share one title.

The Home, Services, Fleet, Gallery, Video Gallery, Blog, About and Contact pages all carry the title “Luxury Limo Service Las Vegas | JMC Limousine”. Every one of the 21 except Cookies and Privacy carries the same description, “Premium Las Vegas limo service for airport pickups, weddings & events…”, including all 11 service pages. Those service pages have one-word titles: Airport, Allegiant, Bachelorette, Bachelor, Concert, Dinner, Downtown, Hopping, LightAndSight, Hourly (the Pregame page) and Wedding. None of the 21 pages has a main heading: your name is a second-level heading on every page, and the headings on the About and Contact pages read “About-us” and “Contact-us”. In my own-name search, your Contact and Services pages showed up under the homepage's title.

04

Your address, names and Facebook links don't line up.

Your Contact page says 3169 Redwood St, Las Vegas, NV, with no ZIP. Your business markup says 3460 Twilight Star Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89177. The same markup lists the accepted currency as EUR, gives prices as “$$$”, still carries an offer that ended December 31, 2025, and states a 5.0 rating from 449 reviews, while the homepage shows 27 individual reviews and no total. Your business also goes by JMC Limousine (header and markup), JMC Transportation (footer and About page) and Jorge Limousine Service (your Instagram, YouTube and TikTok handles). The Facebook icons on every page go to facebook.com/profile.php?id=100077687551861, while your About page links to a different one, the Jorge Limousine Service page.

05

Your Video Gallery shows other people's travel vlogs, not your own films.

I loaded the Video Gallery and counted ten videos. The titles read as visitors' travel vlogs, for example “Vegas Things: Episode 3!”, “10 Years Married in Vegas: From Limo Rides to Dive Bars!” and “Las Vegas April 2025 Travel Day”, and I didn't watch all ten. No film shows on your homepage, Fleet, Gallery or service pages. Your TikTok account (@jorge_limousine_service) lists 162 videos and your YouTube channel has 2 videos and 7 Shorts, none of them on the pages where a visitor decides.

06

Small fixes from my checks.

Your sitemap lists 18 addresses and all 18 load, but each has the same last-changed date, August 10, 2025, the file still contains the notes “Add all services dynamically” and “Add other services”, and the Blog your menu links to isn't in it. A made-up address, /this-page-does-not-exist-xyz, returns a normal page (the homepage) instead of a “not found” answer. Four console errors on the homepage come from your own security rules blocking Google's ad-measurement calls to doubleclick.net, so those conversions may not be recorded. The logo's alt text reads “logo-main”.

What's working

Your phone number is a tap-to-call link on every page, “Available 24/7” sits in the header, and a WhatsApp link is on every page I opened. Your airport page shows prices (from $100 Sunday to Thursday and $125 Friday and Saturday), the Fleet page shows capacity and your license (“Fully Insured and Fully Licensed, CPCN 2306”), and the homepage shows named guest reviews with dates. The homepage scored 100 out of 100 on my accessibility check. Your robots.txt names your sitemap and blocks only /admin/ and /private/. In my search for your name, four of the five results were your own pages and the fifth was your Nevada Transportation Authority filing.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the Book Now button and the contact form deliver, and the Terms page beyond its title. I opened 22 pages. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt blocks only /admin/ and /private/, and it names your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Gap
    Page text is not in the page the server sends

    The homepage HTML the server sends is 1,772 bytes with 0 words of text, no heading, no description and no phone number or address. The text is drawn by a script, so a tool that doesn't run scripts sees an empty page.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

  • Found
    Business markup has the basics (once the script runs)

    After the script runs, your page code describes a local business with its name, phone, 24-hour opening times, English and Spanish, five social links and a rating.

  • Gap
    Markup details conflict with the page

    The markup gives 3460 Twilight Star Dr, 89177, while your Contact page says 3169 Redwood St. It lists the currency as EUR, prices as “$$$”, and an offer that ended on December 31, 2025.

  • Note
    Pages share one description

    19 of the 21 pages I checked carry the same description, and 8 share the same title.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
