What your site is costing you — JMC Limousine —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone planning a party-bus night or an airport pickup would: find you, check the vehicles and prices, call. Your phone is a tap-to-call link on every page, the service pages show starting prices, and the SEO and accessibility checks came back strong. The leaks are a very heavy homepage, two different street addresses, no page heading on any page, and tracking calls your own security settings block.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your homepage loads 119 images and takes about 10 seconds to show its main content on a phone.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage transferred 9,760 KiB in 139 requests, and 119 of those were images. Its main content showed at 9.7 and 10.1 seconds, and the speed score was 47 and 46 out of 100. The Fleet page scored 51, with its main content at 7.8 seconds, and the Video Gallery page scored 68. One test setup, two runs on the homepage; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your Contact page and your business markup give two different street addresses.

Your Contact page says 3169 Redwood St Las Vegas NV. The business markup in your homepage code, which search tools read, says 3460 Twilight Star Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89177. I didn't visit either, so I can't say which one is current.

03

None of your 21 pages has a main heading, and most share one title and description.

I opened 21 pages in a browser and found no H1 heading on any of them. Eight pages, including Home, Services, Fleet, Gallery and Contact, share the title “Luxury Limo Service Las Vegas | JMC Limousine”, and 19 pages share one page description. Your 11 service pages are titled with a single word each, such as “Hopping”, “Hourly” and “LightAndSight”.

04

Your pages are built by script, so the page a crawler downloads has no text.

The homepage file a crawler downloads is 1,772 bytes: an empty container and a script. The words, headings and links only appear after JavaScript runs. Search and AI tools that don't run scripts see none of your content. Google can run scripts, but I can't say how it indexes your pages.

05

Your own security settings block your Google Ads and Analytics calls.

On page load, Chrome logged errors showing it blocked connections to ad.doubleclick.net and stats.g.doubleclick.net, because your site's content-security-policy doesn't allow them. Those are Google Ads and Analytics calls, so some visits and ad conversions may not be recorded. I can't see your Google accounts, so I can't say how much is missing.

06

Your business markup says EUR, “$$$” prices and 449 reviews.

The structured data on your homepage lists the accepted currency as EUR, gives the airport pickup offer a price of “$$$” instead of a number, and states a 5.0 rating from 449 reviews. The homepage itself shows 8 customer reviews and doesn't say where the 449 come from. Search tools may ignore markup that doesn't match the page. I can't say how Google treats yours.

What's working

The accessibility score was 100 on the homepage and the SEO score was 100 on every page I tested. Your phone is a tap-to-call link on every page, your service pages show starting prices (for example airport pickup from $100 Sunday to Thursday and $125 Friday and Saturday, taxes and fees not included), and your Fleet page says “Fully Insured and Fully Licensed, CPCN 2306”. Every main page has a canonical address and business markup, the Contact page has a short form, and your sitemap lists 18 pages.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks on a phone (untested), whether the contact form or the jorge@jmclimousine.com address reach you, your Google listing, and where the 449 reviews in your markup come from. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt blocks only /admin/ and /private/ and lists your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Business markup on every main page

    Your pages carry LocalBusiness markup with your name, phone, hours (open 24 hours), social profiles and an offer list.

  • Found
    Phone is a tap-to-call link

    Every page links your phone as tel:+1-702-969-2763 and shows 702 969-2763 as text.

  • Gap
    Page text depends on scripts

    The raw homepage has an empty container and no words. A crawler that doesn't run JavaScript gets no headings, text or links.

  • Gap
    No page heading and repeated titles

    No H1 on any of 21 pages, and 8 pages share one title, so search and AI tools get few clues about what each page is.

  • Gap
    Markup doesn't match the page

    Address, currency, price and review count in the markup differ from, or aren't backed by, what the pages show.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

  • Note
    Blog and policy pages are missing from the sitemap

    Your sitemap lists 18 pages. The Blog, Cookies Policy and Privacy Policy pages are not in it, and the policy pages have no description or canonical address.

  • Note
    Social profiles use another name

    Your Instagram, TikTok and YouTube links all point to “jorgelimousineservice” or “jorge_limousine_service”, not JMC.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself, if you have one: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.

What I'd fix first

Shrink and lazy-load the homepage images, serve the page text in the HTML itself, add one clear heading and a unique title and description to every page, and pick one street address for the Contact page and the markup. Allow the Google tags in your security settings and make the markup match what the page shows. I can't promise where it will place you.
